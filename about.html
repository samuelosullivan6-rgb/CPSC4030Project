About the project

Levi Dyer, Mir Patel, Samuel Sullivan · CPSC 4030-001 Data Visualization · Fall 2026

CellTrack Explorer is an interactive dashboard for exploring how cells behave in time-lapse microscopy videos. It covers 25 ground-truth sequences of four cell lines (MCF10A, HEK293T, MDA-MB-231, U87), with 229,890 annotated cell positions in all. With it, you can compare how fast cultures grow and how fast cells move, find microscope stage shifts, and follow a single cell's family tree and path.

How to use the dashboard

  1. Overview: scan growth (A) and the stage-shift timeline (B). Vertical stripes in B are microscope events.
  2. Zoom and filter: click a sequence, brush a time window in B or a speed range in E, or click an ending in F.
  3. Details: click a path in C to open its lineage in D, then hover branches to see their paths.
